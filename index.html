HazardGuard Drone

AI-Powered Safety Decision System for Residential Drone Delivery

A human-centered AI safety layer that continuously assesses the delivery area, detects changing hazards, and decides whether delivery should proceed, wait, or stop.

Project Overview

Safety at the delivery point — not only during flight.

⚠️ The Problem

Residential delivery areas can change while a drone approaches or prepares to release a package. A person, child, animal, vehicle, or obstacle may enter the interaction zone unexpectedly.

Our approach
Instead of treating detection as the final step, HazardGuard turns visual information into a safety decision.

“Is it safe to continue the delivery right now?”

Safety Status

A simple decision layer translates the scene into an action.

SAFE

No relevant hazard is detected in the delivery zone. The process may continue.

WAIT

A temporary or uncertain condition is present. Delivery pauses while the system reassesses.

STOP

A significant hazard is detected. The delivery is held or aborted until conditions are acceptable.

Innovation & Safety Decision Layer

The core contribution is the connection between perception, risk assessment and action.

The innovation
The safety decision layer connects visual perception and risk assessment to an operational response such as continuing, waiting, holding, rerouting or aborting the delivery.

How It Works

From visual input to an explainable delivery decision.

01

Monitor

Camera input observes the delivery area.

02

Detect

AI vision identifies people, animals, vehicles and obstacles.

03

Track

Detected objects are followed across frames to understand movement.

04

Assess

Position, movement and proximity to the delivery path are evaluated.

05

Estimate

Potential path conflicts are estimated from current movement, distance and route geometry.

06

Decide

The safety engine selects an action and explains why.

07

Guide

The recipient is directed to the designated interaction area when appropriate.

08

Recheck

The scene is reassessed before collection and before departure.

09

Depart

The drone resumes its route only after the area is confirmed safe.

Evidence & Judge Guide

What the current prototype demonstrates, how the decision is made, and what remains future work.

01 · What is demonstrated?

The interactive browser prototype visibly demonstrates moving hazards, distance, predicted distance, closing speed, relative speed, time-to-collision (TTC), risk level, safety status, action, route state, replans and an event log.

02 · Why does the decision change?

The safety decision combines proximity, relative movement, estimated short-horizon conflict and route clearance. The result is translated into a simple SAFE / WAIT / STOP state and an operational action.

03 · What is the innovation?

The project focuses on the safety decision layer: perception is not treated as the final output. Information is converted into a safety response such as continue, wait, hold, reroute or abort.

04 · What can a judge test?

  • Open the interactive simulation.
  • Observe independent moving hazards.
  • Watch risk and action change as conditions change.
  • Use Force Reroute and Reset to inspect the workflow.

05 · What is not claimed?

The current work does not claim a deployed autonomous drone, real-flight validation, guaranteed collision avoidance, measured field accuracy, or commercial readiness.

06 · How will it be validated?

Future controlled testing can measure detection and tracking performance, response time, decision consistency, false alerts, missed hazards and route-safety behavior across repeatable scenarios.

Prototype scope: This submission is a software proof of concept. The browser simulator is evidence of the proposed decision workflow, while real-drone integration and field validation remain future development.

💡 My Second Idea: Smart Safe

An independent future concept for secure package storage and pickup.

SMART SAFE
SEPARATE FUTURE CONCEPT
🚀 Future concept only — no physical smart safe has been built or deployed yet.

🔐 The Idea

A smart storage station that receives the delivered package, assigns it to a secure compartment, verifies the recipient, and records the pickup. This is a separate concept from the HazardGuard Drone safety system.

⚙️ How It Works

01

🛸 Drone Arrival

The drone reaches the designated delivery area and starts the package handoff process.

02

📦 Package Registration

The system records the shipment and assigns it a unique tracking reference.

03

🔐 Compartment Assignment

A suitable secure compartment is assigned to the package.

04

👁️ Package Monitoring

The package remains secured while its storage status is monitored.

05

🔑 Recipient Verification

The recipient receives a mobile notification with a secure one-time opening code and uses it to verify access.

06

✅ Safe Opening

After successful verification, only the assigned compartment is opened.

07

📝 Pickup Record

The system records that the package was collected and updates its status.

08

🔄 Ready Again

The compartment returns to an available state for a future delivery.

ARRIVED → ASSIGNED → NOTIFIED → VERIFIED → OPENED → PICKED UP

🤖 My Third Idea: Smart Delivery Robot

An independent future concept for secure autonomous ground delivery.

🚀 Future concept only — no physical robot or hardware prototype has been built or deployed yet.

🚚 The Idea

A mobile delivery robot that receives a package from a smart safe, verifies the delivery task, travels independently to the recipient home, and releases the package only after secure recipient verification.

⚙️ How It Works

01

🔐 Safe Pickup

The robot arrives at the smart safe and collects the assigned package.

02

📦 Secure Loading

The package is placed inside the protected delivery compartment.

03

🔑 Task Verification

The robot verifies the delivery task and secure access information before departure.

04

🧠 AI Navigation

Cameras, sensors and AI monitor people, vehicles, animals and obstacles while the robot moves.

05

🛑 Safety Stop

If a vehicle, person or other hazard creates an unsafe condition, the robot stops and reassesses.

06

🔄 Safe Rerouting

When possible, the system selects a safer alternative path instead of continuing through the hazard.

07

🏠 Home Arrival

The robot reaches the designated delivery location and prepares the package for handoff.

08

🔐 Secure Handoff

The recipient enters a one-time security code, and the protected delivery box opens only after successful verification.

SAFE PICKUP → AI NAVIGATION → SAFETY STOP / REROUTE → HOME → CODE VERIFIED → PACKAGE RELEASED
