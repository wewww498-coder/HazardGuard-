HazardGuard Drone

AI-Powered Safety Decision System for Residential Drone Delivery

A human-centered AI safety layer that continuously assesses the delivery area, detects changing hazards, and decides whether delivery should proceed, wait, or stop.

View Simulation How It Works

Project Overview

Safety at the delivery point — not only during flight.

⚠️ The Problem

Residential delivery areas can change while a drone approaches or prepares to release a package. A person, child, animal, vehicle, or obstacle may enter the interaction zone unexpectedly.

Our approach
Instead of treating detection as the final step, HazardGuard turns visual information into a safety decision:

“Is it safe to continue the delivery right now?”

Safety Status

A simple decision layer translates the scene into an action.

SAFE

No relevant hazard is detected in the delivery zone. The process may continue.

WAIT

A temporary or uncertain condition is present. Delivery pauses while the system reassesses.

STOP

A significant hazard is detected. The delivery is held or aborted until conditions are acceptable.

How It Works

From visual input to an explainable delivery decision.

01

Monitor

Camera input observes the delivery area.

02

Detect

AI vision identifies people, animals, vehicles and obstacles.

03

Track

Detected objects are followed across frames to understand movement.

04

Assess

The system considers position, movement and proximity to the delivery path.

05

Predict

Potential path conflicts are estimated before the next action.

06

Decide

The safety engine selects SAFE, WAIT or STOP and explains why.

07

Guide

The recipient is directed to the designated interaction area when appropriate.

08

Recheck

The scene is reassessed before collection and again before departure.

09

Depart

The drone resumes its route only after the area is confirmed safe.

Latest Programming Development

The latest software work completed for the HazardGuard Drone proof of concept.

📏 Distance Calculation

Programming was developed to calculate and evaluate the distance between the drone and detected people, vehicles, animals and obstacles, so the decision is based on proximity rather than detection alone.

🧠 Risk Analysis Engine

The safety logic combines object type, distance, movement and route conflict to determine the current level of delivery risk.

🚦 Safety Decisions

The system can issue SAFE, WAIT, STOP, HOLD, AVOID or ABORT DELIVERY decisions according to changing conditions.

🎯 Object Tracking

Detected objects are tracked across video frames to monitor movement and identify changing conditions around the drone.

🛣️ Dynamic Rerouting

When the planned path becomes unsafe, the simulation can evaluate a safer route and change the drone path before a predicted conflict.

📍 GPS & Mission State

The simulator includes GPS position and mission-state logic so the drone can move through takeoff, navigation, delivery, return and landing stages.

📡 Signal & Battery Checks

Signal and battery conditions are included in the simulated mission monitoring instead of treating the flight path as the only safety factor.

📦 Package Logic

The package remains attached during the mission, is handed over at the delivery point, and the drone then performs a safety recheck before returning to base.

📝 Movement Log

Important movement, hazard and decision events can be recorded to make the simulation easier to inspect and evaluate.

👤 Recipient Safety

The concept includes guidance toward a designated safe collection position and pauses the delivery when the interaction area is not safe.

🔄 Full Mission Flow

Depot → package loading → takeoff → AI route → hazard monitoring → delivery → post-delivery safety check → return-to-base → landing → completion.

📡 Mission Status Monitoring
