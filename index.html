HazardGuard Drone

AI-Powered Safety Decision System for Residential Drone Delivery

A human-centered AI safety layer that continuously assesses the delivery area, detects changing hazards, and decides whether delivery should proceed, wait, or stop.

View Simulation How It Works

Project Overview

Safety at the delivery point — not only during flight.

⚠️ The Problem

Residential delivery areas can change while a drone approaches or prepares to release a package. A person, child, animal, vehicle, or obstacle may enter the interaction zone unexpectedly.

Our approach
Instead of treating detection as the final step, HazardGuard turns visual information into a safety decision:

“Is it safe to continue the delivery right now?”

Safety Status

A simple decision layer translates the scene into an action.

SAFE

No relevant hazard is detected in the delivery zone. The process may continue.

WAIT

A temporary or uncertain condition is present. Delivery pauses while the system reassesses.

STOP

A significant hazard is detected. The delivery is held or aborted until conditions are acceptable.

How It Works

From visual input to an explainable delivery decision.

01

Monitor

Camera input observes the delivery area.

02

Detect

AI vision identifies people, animals, vehicles and obstacles.

03

Track

Detected objects are followed across frames to understand movement.

04

Assess

The system considers position, movement and proximity to the delivery path.

05

Predict

Potential path conflicts are estimated before the next action.

06

Decide

The safety engine selects SAFE, WAIT or STOP and explains why.

07

Guide

The recipient is directed to the designated interaction area when appropriate.

08

Recheck

The scene is reassessed before collection and again before departure.

09

Depart

The drone resumes its route only after the area is confirmed safe.

Expected Results

What the proposed safety layer is designed to demonstrate.

Human Safety

Reduce potentially unsafe human–drone interactions during package collection.

Dynamic Awareness

Respond to changing conditions instead of relying on a fixed delivery-area assumption.

Controlled Delivery

Pause, hold, or resume the delivery according to the current scene.

Explainable Action

Connect each safety decision to a clear action and reason.

Future Development

From software simulation toward controlled prototype testing.

Future work will focus on real-time camera integration, stronger multi-object tracking, path-risk prediction, recipient guidance, controlled drone integration, and measured evaluation using detection accuracy, response time, false alerts, missed hazards, and decision consistency.

From object detection to safety decisions.

Object detection answers “what is in the image?” HazardGuard adds “what should happen next?”
