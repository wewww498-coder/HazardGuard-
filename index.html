HazardGuard Drone

AI-Powered Safety Decision System for Residential Drone Delivery

A human-centered AI safety layer designed to continuously assess the delivery area, detect dynamic hazards, and decide whether delivery should proceed, wait, or stop.

View SimulationHow It Works
01 · OVERVIEW

Safety at the delivery point — not only during flight.

Residential drone delivery brings the aircraft close to people and changing environments. HazardGuard adds a safety decision layer that evaluates the final human–drone interaction before package collection.

The system continuously asks:

“Is it safe to continue the delivery right now?”
02 · THE PROBLEM

Dynamic environments require dynamic safety decisions.

A delivery area can become unsafe seconds after it was clear. People, children, animals, vehicles, and obstacles may enter while the drone is approaching or preparing to release a package.

The safety gap

Navigation can tell a drone where to go. HazardGuard focuses on a different question: whether the environment is safe for the human–drone interaction at the delivery point.

Challenge: Detect a changing hazard and translate it into a clear delivery action before an unsafe interaction occurs.
👤

People & children

Unexpected movement near the delivery area.

🐕

Animals

Animals entering or crossing the relevant area.

🚗

Vehicles & obstacles

Moving or static objects that can affect the delivery condition.

03 · SAFETY STATUS

Three simple operational states.

🟢

SAFE

The relevant area is sufficiently clear. Continue the delivery process.

🟡

WAIT

A temporary condition requires observation. Hold delivery while monitoring continues.

🔴

STOP

A significant hazard is detected. Pause delivery and reassess the environment.

04 · HOW IT WORKS

Detect → Track → Assess → Decide → Guide

The prototype connects computer vision with safety-oriented decision logic.

01 · MONITOR

Monitor

A camera observes the residential delivery environment.

02 · DETECT

Detect

AI vision identifies people, children, animals, vehicles, and obstacles.

03 · TRACK

Track

Detected objects are tracked as their positions change.

04 · ASSESS

Assess

The system evaluates object position relative to the delivery safety area.

05 · PREDICT

Predict

Movement is considered to estimate whether an object may enter or interfere with the relevant area.

06 · DECIDE

Decide

The safety engine selects SAFE, WAIT, or STOP.

07 · GUIDE

Guide

When appropriate, the recipient is directed toward a designated safe position.

08 · RECHECK

Recheck

The environment is checked again before and after collection.

09 · DEPART

Depart

The drone proceeds with departure after the final safety check.

📷 CAMERA→🧠 AI VISION→🎯 DETECTION & TRACKING→⚠️ RISK ASSESSMENT→🛡️ DECISION→🚁 DELIVERY / HOLD / STOP
05 · DIFFERENTIATOR

From object detection to safety decisions.

Object detection answers “what is in the image?” HazardGuard adds the next question: “what should happen next?”

The prototype connects object location, movement, safety-zone proximity, and delivery status to a clear operational response.

06 · PROTOTYPE & SIMULATION

Software prototype and simulation

The current prototype demonstrates the proposed safety workflow through AI-based visual analysis and simulation. It is not presented as a deployed commercial drone system.

07 · EXPECTED RESULTS

Safety outcomes the system aims to support

01 · HUMAN SAFETY

Reduce potentially unsafe human–drone interactions during package collection.

02 · DYNAMIC AWARENESS

Respond to changing conditions rather than relying only on a fixed delivery state.

03 · CONTROLLED DELIVERY

Hold or stop delivery when safety conditions are not satisfied.

04 · EXPLAINABLE ACTION

Provide a clear safety state and corresponding action.

09 · PROJECT STATUS

Independent Student Innovation

Current stage: concept + AI software prototype + simulation. The project does not claim that the complete system has already been deployed on a physical delivery drone or used in commercial residential operations.

Safety before delivery.

A successful delivery is not only a package reaching its destination — it is a package being delivered without creating an unsafe interaction.

Detect the hazard. Assess the situation. Protect the person. Then deliver.
